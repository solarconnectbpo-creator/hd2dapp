# Chicago field hiring plan

Created: 2026-10-15

Stand up a Chicagoland door-to-door closer pod this winter so the team is trained and knocking when the spring window opens. Target: **12 launched reps in 90 days**, with **~8 still active at day 30** and **~5 still producing at day 90**.

This is an operations plan, not a software change. It is written for Hardcore Door to Door Closers / Solar Connect: a home-improvement sales shop that already recruits remote solar closers and now needs local Chicago field capacity.

## Assumptions

These bets are locked so the plan can be executed without waiting. Change any one of them and the funnel, offer, and calendar move with it.

| Bet | Locked choice | If this is wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Field canvasser / closer in Chicagoland, not more remote phone closers | Use the existing Solar Connect apply path and hire against CST hours instead |
| Vertical | Solar plus roofing / exteriors | Drop the unused product from the job ad and training week |
| Starting point | No dedicated Chicago office or recruiter | Hire a player-coach first and rent a morning huddle spot |
| Headcount | First pod of 12 launched, not a 40-rep office | Recalculate the weekly interview quota |
| Pay | W-2 outside sales, training draw + uncapped commission | 1099 or straight commission-only will lose candidates and add legal risk |
| Territory | Collar suburbs first (DuPage, Lake, Will, Kane, northwest Cook) | City-proper knocking is slower, more parking friction, and more ordinance work |

## Why this calendar works

Today is 15 October 2026. Chicagoland field sales is a warm-weather machine.

- Hail / wind roofing canvassing in the collar counties runs roughly **April through October**.
- Solar knocking is strongest **spring through fall**, with the late-afternoon weekday window (about 4–7pm) and Saturday 10am–4pm.
- Competitors are already hiring Chicago-area canvassers and roofing closers in January.

Hiring only when the first storm hits means you spend peak season training instead of selling. Recruit through winter. Launch the field team in late March.

Winter is not dead time. Use it for interviews, ride-alongs, phone / warm-lead closing, and storm-restoration work if a cell hits.

## Success signals

The plan is working when all of these are true by **13 January 2027** (90 days from today):

- 12 people have completed day 1 in the field or on a winter training route
- 8 of those 12 are still showing up at day 30
- At least 1 named Chicago player-coach is running morning huddle and same-day offers
- Ads and interviews run every week, not in bursts
- Every active suburb on the knock list has a solicitor-permit plan (approved, in process, or explicitly skipped)

A useful later checkpoint, not a 90-day gate: **~5 still producing at day 90**. Cold D2D recruiting often keeps about 20% of launched reps at that mark. If you are under 3, the problem is usually onboarding or the offer, not the ad.

## Funnel math

Work backward from 5 day-90 producers using conservative cold-recruiting rates (about 70% of accepted offers launch, 40% of launches remain at day 30, 20% at day 90):

| Stage | 90-day count | Weekly pace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applications | 400 | ~45 |
| Phone screens | 120 | ~14 |
| Interviews held | 72 | ~8 |
| Offers accepted | 36 | ~4 |
| Launched (day 1) | 12 | keep a running total |
| Active at day 30 | ~8 | — |
| Producing at day 90 | ~5 | — |

Interview-to-offer should sit near 50%. If it is much lower, the ad is attracting the wrong people or the interview is selling poorly. If launch rate is under 60%, the gap is between “yes” and first day — call the new hire within 30 minutes and get them on a calendar inside 72 hours.

## Offer that can win in this market

Chicago competitors are advertising:

- Solar / D2D: roughly **$65k–$150k OTE**, often with a training draw
- Roofing / restoration: **$80k–$200k+**, sometimes a base or draw for experienced people
- Appointment closers (Erie Home, Lombard): first-year averages around **$85k–$135k**, weekly pay

Your published offer should look at least this clean:

- **W-2**, not 1099
- **$500–$750/week recoverable draw** for the first 4 weeks (or $400 + mileage if cash is tight)
- Uncapped commission with a written example: “3 deals / month = about $X”
- **Weekly pay**
- Fast-start: **$250 on first sale**, **$500 at 3 sales**
- Referral: **$500** paid when the referred hire hits day 30
- Gas / necessary-expense reimbursement (Illinois requires this when the spend is necessary and submitted on time)
- Phone, CRM login, and a real morning huddle — not “figure it out”

Do not bury “commission only, no training, start tomorrow” in the ad. That copy fills inboxes and empties Tuesday’s interview room.

### Legal notes (not legal advice)

- Illinois and Chicago both have an **outside-sales exemption** from minimum wage when the person’s main job is making sales or taking orders **away from the office**. Door-to-door at the homeowner’s house is the classic case.
- Chicago’s city minimum wage is **$17.05/hour as of 1 July 2026** for employers with 4+ people. It applies to people who work at least 2 hours in the city in a two-week period **unless** they qualify as outside sales. Phone closers sitting in a Chicago apartment generally do **not** qualify.
- Illinois statewide minimum wage is **$15/hour**.
- City of Chicago’s peddler license is for **street merchandise / carts**, not typical residential canvassing. Collar suburbs are stricter. **Naperville** requires a solicitor permit, police background check, and badge: **$100 / 30 days** or **$300 / 90 days**, Monday–Saturday 10am–7pm, no Sundays or holidays. Door hangers do not need a permit there. Build a permit checklist before you knock a town, not after a police stop.
- Paystubs are required. Final pay including commissions is due on the next regular payday.

Have counsel confirm classification, draw recovery, and the suburb list before the first offer letter goes out.

## Where to recruit

Run all five at once. Referrals and local video beat job boards on quality. Indeed beats everything on raw volume.

| Channel | Job | Weekly target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee / owner referrals | Best producers | 3 names | Pay $500 at day 30. Text the team every Monday. |
| Indeed | Volume | 25–30 applicants | Sponsor the post. Indeed listed ~11,000 Chicago sales-rep profiles as of 25 Sep 2026. |
| Craigslist Chicago (`sls` + `gig`) | Fast local | 10 applicants | Energy, canvassing, and flyer gigs are already live. Same-day text. |
| Instagram / TikTok / Facebook (Chicago + collar geos) | Athletes, students, career-switchers | 8 applicants | 20–30s videos of huddle, a knock, and a paycheck story. |
| In-person | Gyms, community colleges, military / athlete groups | 2 conversations | College of DuPage, Harper, Joliet Junior College, UIC intramurals. |

Poach language, not people illegally. Mirror what Zeo, Allied Emergency Services, Fresh Page, Erie Home, and Renewal by Andersen are promising — then beat them on **speed to offer**, **weekly pay**, and **a named coach**.

### Job titles that get applications

Do not lead with “door-to-door.” Use:

- Outside Sales Representative
- Entry-Level Sales Associate
- Home Improvement Sales (Training Provided)
- Canvasser / Appointment Setter (Chicago suburbs)

Put door-to-door in the **first three lines** of the body so nobody is surprised.

### Ad body (use this)

> Chicago / collar suburbs. W-2. Training draw $500–$750/week for 4 weeks, then uncapped commission. Weekly pay. Most people who stay hit $80k–$150k. You will knock homes, set inspections, and close. We train you. Driver’s license, reliable car, and a phone. Interviews this week. Text NAME at PHONE to start.

## Who to hire first

Hire in this order. Do not skip the player-coach.

1. **Player-coach (week 1–2).** One person who has sold D2D in a cold market, will run huddle at 9:30am, interview at 11am and 5:30pm, and knock in the afternoon. This hire is the plan. Without them, you are recruiting into a vacuum.
2. **2 experienced closers (weeks 2–5).** Roofing, solar, pest, alarms, or windows. They become trainers and proof in the ad.
3. **8–10 trainable athletes (weeks 3–12).** Team sports, military, restaurant closers, gym staff. Coachable beats “I sold solar in Arizona.”

Screen for: car + insurance, can work 2–8pm and Saturdays, lives inside a 40-minute drive of the huddle point, will knock in 35°F, no “I only want inbound.”

Disqualify fast: no car, wants remote-only, cannot interview within 48 hours, argues with the hours.

## Interview machine

Treat recruiting like a sales pipeline. Same-day response. Offer inside 24 hours of a good interview.

**Daily loop (owner or player-coach):**

1. All new applicants get a text inside 30 minutes: “This is NAME with HD2D Chicago. 10-min call today at 3 or 6?”
2. 10-minute phone screen: car, hours, money goal, “are you willing to knock doors in the suburbs?”
3. Interview the same day or next: 20 minutes in person at the huddle spot, or a parking-lot / coffee shop if you do not have an office yet.
4. Sell the opportunity the way you sell a homeowner: income example, first-week calendar, who their coach is.
5. Offer on the spot. “Training starts Thursday 9:30am at ADDRESS. I’ll text you the pin when we hang up.”
6. Coach calls within 30 minutes. First day inside 72 hours.

**Group interview, twice a week (Tue 11am, Thu 5:30pm).** 45 minutes. 4–8 people. Stand-up pitch, pair drill, one-on-one close. This is how you get to 8 interviews a week without living on the phone.

## 90-day calendar

No day/week effort estimates — only sequence.

### Phase 0 — This week (lock the machine)

- Name the huddle point (rented suite, church basement, or consistent cafe / parking lot in west or northwest suburbs — Naperville, Downers Grove, Schaumburg, or Arlington Heights beat the Loop)
- Write the offer letter, draw rules, and one-page scoreboard
- Post Indeed + Craigslist + one short video
- Text every current rep / friend for referrals
- Book the first two group-interview slots
- Start the suburb permit spreadsheet: Naperville, Aurora, Joliet, Schaumburg, Arlington Heights, Bolingbrook, Orland Park, Wheaton, Elmhurst, Palatine
- Decide the winter work: warm solar leads, storm restoration, or both

### Phase 1 — Weeks 2–6 (hire the core)

- Land the player-coach
- Keep ads live; refresh Indeed copy every 10 days
- Run both group interviews every week even if only 2 people confirm
- Launch the first 4 people on a winter route (phone + weekend knocking when weather allows)
- Pay the first referral bonus in public so the rest of the team copies it

### Phase 2 — Weeks 7–10 (February surge)

- Raise Indeed spend if applications fall under 30/week
- Add a second huddle time for people who work days
- Convert the 2 experienced closers into interviewers
- File solicitor permits for the March territory list so badges are in hand before the first warm Saturday

### Phase 3 — Weeks 11–13 (March launch)

- Full field schedule: weekday 4–7pm, Saturday 10–4
- Cut anyone who will not hold a door count
- Freeze new-hire class size at what the player-coach can ride with (usually 3–4 rookies at a time)
- Start the next 90-day pod only after day-30 retention is at least 40%

## Cash to stand the pod up

Working numbers, not a budget ask. Adjust the draw if the offer changes.

| Item | Cash |
| --- | --- |
| Training draws (12 people × $600 × 4 weeks) | ~$29,000 |
| Ads (Indeed + Meta, 12 weeks × ~$700) | ~$8,400 |
| Referral bonuses (8 × $500) | $4,000 |
| Fast-start bonuses (first sales) | $2,000–$4,000 |
| Solicitor permits (estimate, 8 towns × a few badges) | $2,000–$5,000 |
| Huddle space / shirts / door materials | $1,500 |
| **Working total** | **~$47,000–$52,000** |

The expensive line is the draw. That is also what keeps rookies through week two in a Chicago January. If cash is tight, hire 6 launched instead of 12 and keep the draw. Do not cut the draw to fund more ads.

## Scoreboard (track weekly)

- Applicants by source
- Phone screens held
- Interviews held / no-show rate
- Offers out / accepted
- Launches
- Day-7, day-30, day-90 still active
- First sale date
- Cost per launched hire
- Cost per day-30 hire

Kill a channel that does not produce a launch in 21 days. Double the channel that produced the last two day-30 reps.

## Risks and contingencies

| Risk | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Player-coach hire slips | Owner runs both group interviews and one ride-along until week 4. Do not pause ads. |
| January weather kills knocking | Shift rookies to phone blocks and Saturday-only doors. Keep launching people. |
| Indeed quality is junk | Tighten the first three lines of the ad. Require a car in the screener. Move spend to referrals + video. |
| Suburb police stop a rookie | Pull that town off the list until the badge is in hand. Door-hanger only in the meantime (Naperville allows this without a permit). |
| Day-30 retention under 30% | Stop hiring for one week. Fix huddle, draw, and the first-sale path. Volume on a broken ramp wastes the draw budget. |
| You actually wanted remote Chicago closers | Keep the interview machine and offer. Drop permits, cars, and evening knock windows. Hire against CST from the existing Solar Connect apply flow. |
